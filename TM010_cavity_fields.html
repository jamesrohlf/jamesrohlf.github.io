TM010 cavity fields

The lowest mode of a cylindrical cavity. The electric field runs along the axis and the magnetic field circles it, a quarter cycle apart, so the energy moves out to the magnetic field and back twice per cycle.

Nothing travels. This is a standing wave: the pattern stays put and only its size and sign change. It is the sum of an inward and an outward cylindrical wave, and their energy flows cancel on average. Turn on Energy flow to see what is left, a radial sloshing at twice the frequency.

Frequency depends only on the radius, f = 2.405 c / 2πR, not on the length, because nothing varies along z. Presets A, B and C are the DAPHNE radii with the cavity empty; the tuning rod raises each by 7% or more.
