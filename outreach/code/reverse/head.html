Reverse Engineering the CMB

Every other page here runs the cosmology forwards: choose the parameters, compute the curve. This one runs it backwards. Take the Planck measurement, lay a ruler across it, and see how far five numbers read off a graph will carry you — to the densities of matter and of ordinary atoms, to the size of a sound wave frozen 380,000 years after the Big Bang, to the distance light has travelled since, and finally to the expansion rate of the universe today.
