Baryon Acoustic Oscillations

The imprint of sound waves in the primordial plasma survives as a preferred separation between galaxies — a standard ruler roughly 150 Mpc long. Measuring it at a range of redshifts maps the expansion history.

DESI measures it two ways: from the clustering of discrete tracers out to z ≈ 2.1, and from Lyman-α forest absorption at z ≈ 2.3. The cosmological constraints drawn from these measurements are collected under Cosmological Parameter Estimation. Forest catalogs, systematics and the pipeline reference set are on the Lyman-α page.
